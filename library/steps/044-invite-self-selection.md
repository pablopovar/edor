Turn the buyer-recognition brief in the **Material** and the **Original Material** into one complete sales asset that helps the reader recognize the current situation, understand why reconsideration may be worthwhile, see what a different outcome would make possible, understand how the offer contributes, evaluate evidence, limitations, tradeoffs, and fit, and decide whether to continue.

Do not exaggerate the problem, force a conclusion, append alternatives, or include analysis or process commentary.

Use that progression to improve the **Material** and provide one complete reformulated **Material** ending with a clear, proportionate invitation that allows the reader to continue, decline, or determine that the offer is not for them.
