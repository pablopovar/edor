Develop minimal **Material**, such as an idea, sentence, fragment, or rough outline, into sufficiently complete source material for an existing drafting Step or stack.

Make its central direction, relevant substance, distinctions, implications, and necessary support explicit without attempting to write the finished draft.

Use your conclusions to improve the **Material** and provide one complete reformulated **Material** ready for drafting.
