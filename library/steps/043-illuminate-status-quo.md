enabled: true

Identify and explain, from the buyer's point of view, what they are trying to accomplish, how they handle it now, where friction, uncertainty, delay, risk, or unnecessary effort occurs, why it matters, what they may not have considered, and what remains unsupported or unresolved.

Do not pitch the offer, manufacture urgency, or assume every buyer has the problem.

Use your conclusions to improve the **Material** and provide one complete reformulated **Material** as a concise buyer-recognition brief grounded in the available facts and the buyer's natural language.
