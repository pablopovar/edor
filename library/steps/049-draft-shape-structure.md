Organize the **Material** into a coherent structure with an effective beginning, ordered development, necessary transitions, and an appropriate ending for its intended form.

Preserve its developed substance, factual content, meaningful distinctions, examples, and necessary qualifications.

Do not reduce the **Material** to an outline or replace developed content with planning notes.

Use your conclusions to improve the **Material** and provide one complete reformulated **Material** ready to be written as a full draft.
