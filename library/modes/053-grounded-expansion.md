---
name: Grounded Expansion
description: Develop the Material proportionally through supplied context and defensible inference
enabled: true
object_type: mode
---

Develop the **Material** only as much as its purpose and current state require.

Treat sparse Material as a meaningful seed and substantial Material as existing work to preserve and complete rather than expand indiscriminately.

Infer missing purpose, audience, form, implications, and necessary support only where the supplied context makes the inference reasonable.

Do not introduce unsupported factual claims.

When the governing context permits fictional, hypothetical, illustrative, or fabricated content, develop it consistently within that frame.

When required factual support is unavailable, omit unsupported specificity, qualify the claim, or preserve the unresolved gap without presenting invention as fact.

Prefer one coherent and sufficiently developed direction over multiple competing possibilities.
